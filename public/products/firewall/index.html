Rafiq Firewall

It learns what normal traffic looks like for each app and device, blocks the usual suspicious activity out of the box, and helps you write rules in plain language.

See how it works

Pick a step.

What it does

  • Shows what is talking to whom, per app and per device.
  • Blocks known-bad activity with curated, signed rule packs, and gives a reason for every block.
  • Learns a baseline for each device and flags departures from it.
  • Onboards a new device with one card: what it seems to be, what it tried to reach, and a sensible set of rules to approve, narrow or deny.
  • Runs on a laptop or desktop first, then on the Hub as the gateway for the whole home.

Your data

It records connection details such as addresses, names, sizes and timing. It never stores the contents of your traffic. The models run on your own hardware, and nothing is reported anywhere unless you opt in.

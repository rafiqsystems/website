Lamha

A touch-first way to read and mark up PDFs, images and notes, built once for Android, iOS, iPadOS, Linux, Windows and macOS.

What it does

  • View PDFs and images, with search, page tools and form filling.
  • Mark up with ink, shapes, highlights and text. Stylus pressure is supported.
  • Notebooks with paper templates, a text and Markdown editor, and photo markup.
  • Opens files through the system picker, the share sheet or "open with". It never scans your storage.
  • Adapts to foldables, tablets and desktop windows.

Your data

Everything happens on the device. Export strips location data from images by default, and sharing goes through the system share sheet only. Optional sync to a self-hosted server, end-to-end encrypted, is planned.

Status

In development. Viewing, markup, forms, notebooks and the editors are built. Encrypted sync and sharing come later.

Rafiq Autonomous Car System

Rafiq in each car. Cars that run it share hazards, intentions and congestion with one another, so each driver learns sooner what the cars ahead already know.

What it does

  • Warnings about what you cannot see yet: a stopped vehicle around the bend, sudden braking ahead, a reported hazard.
  • Route, departure and speed advice that keeps traffic flowing for everyone, not just one car.
  • Suggestions at merges and junctions.
  • Standard message formats, so it can interoperate with existing vehicle-to-vehicle systems.

Your data

What leaves the car is a small, signed, short-lived message you can read. Identities rotate every few minutes, shared knowledge travels as aggregates, and no party outside the car holds a record of where it has been.

Status

Later, from the second half of 2028. Designed, not yet built. The first releases only advise the driver. Anything that influences steering, braking or acceleration waits for a safety case and a certified vehicle interface.

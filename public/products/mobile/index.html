Jawwal

The companion on Android and iOS: chat, voice, your study material and your daily briefing, working without a cloud account.

See how it works

Pick a step.

What it does

  • Chat and voice with a small model that runs on the phone.
  • Your briefing, flashcards and lecture player on the go.
  • End-to-end encrypted sync with your own Markaz.

Your data

The phone holds its own encrypted copy of your data. Sync passes through a relay that only ever sees ciphertext, and you can run that relay yourself.

Majlis

MAJ-liss·nounthe room where a household gathers

One shared screen for the household: your own film and show library, your photos, and each person's day behind their own profile.

See how it works

Pick a step.

What it does

  • Library and title pages with resume, trailers, cast, reviews and a parents' guide.
  • Playback with high-quality video and surround sound passed straight to your equipment.
  • Profiles opened with a short pattern on the remote, so a shared screen still keeps mail and calendars personal.
  • Each person's day: a short brief, mail, calendar and messages.
  • Photos: memories, albums, people and places from the household library.

Your data

Everything personal comes from the household's own server over the home network. Mail is shown without fetching anything from the internet, so a sender cannot tell that a message was opened. It plays what is already in your library and does not search for or download titles.

Where it stands

Incubating: it runs in one household today and is not yet a supported product. It has not been connected to Qalb yet.

Products

Every product is a surface on one shared core, and each is held to the same principles: local by default, consent for every data flow, and data you can take with you.

Chat · Phase 1

Najwa

NAJ-wa · a confiding conversation

A private chatbot that runs on your own machine and tells you which model answered and where.

 Study · Phase 2

Hikma

HIK-mah · wisdom

Lessons become transcripts, flashcards and a tutor that cites its sources, with study plans and a clear view for parents.

 Assistant · Phase 3

Najid

NAH-jid · one who comes to your help

A personal assistant for your day, with a grant behind every data source.

 Mobile · Phase 3

Jawwal

jaw-WAHL · on the move

Rafiq on your phone, with small models on the device and encrypted sync to your own Markaz.

 Documents · In development

Lamha

LAM-ha · a glance

A document viewer and editor for phone, tablet and desktop, with no network and no accounts.

 Video · In development

Manzar

MAN-zar · a view

A video player for your own files, on the phone and on Android Auto, with no internet permission at all.

 Desktop · Phase 4

Nizam

nee-ZAHM · order; a system

An AI-first desktop: one command surface where you say what you want and agents do it, with every permission visible.

 Firewall · Phase 4

Hisn

HISS-n · a fortress

A next-generation firewall with on-device AI that explains itself, for your laptop, desktop and home.

 Cooperative cars · Later

Qafila

KAH-fi-la · a caravan, travelling together

Cars that cooperate to avoid accidents and traffic, with no central map of who is where.

 TV · Incubating

Majlis

MAJ-liss · the room where a household gathers

A living-room front end for Android TV and Google TV, fed by the household's own server.

 Home · Later

Dar

DAHR · home

Your home's own brain: devices, power, cameras and groceries, run in the house and connected to everything else Rafiq does.

 Finance · Later

Mizan

mee-ZAHN · a balance; a scale

All your accounts, cards and loans in one private ledger, with advice that knows your situation.

 Core · Foundation

Qalb

KALB · heart

The shared engine behind every Rafiq product: models, memory, permissions and an audit log.

 Home server · Foundation

Markaz

MAR-kaz · the centre

Qalb as a one-command server for a household, a school or a team.

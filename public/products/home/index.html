Rafiq Home

A home-automation hub that runs on your own server. Control the house in plain language, manage your power, keep an eye on the cameras and never run out of milk, without a vendor's cloud in the middle.

See how it works

Pick a step.

What it does

  • Lights, heating, locks, sensors and appliances in one place, working with the smart devices you already own.
  • Say what you want, or let Rafiq propose an automation and show you exactly what it would do before it runs.
  • Power: solar, battery, grid and what is using what, with suggestions for when to run the heavy loads and a plan for outages.
  • Cameras that record and detect inside the house. Footage is not uploaded anywhere.
  • Groceries: what is running low, the shopping list, and what you can cook tonight.
  • Part of the suite: cameras on the TV, the house in your morning briefing, new devices kept in check by the Firewall, energy and grocery costs in your budget.

Your data

Everything runs on your Hub. Unlocking a door, opening the garage or switching off an alarm always needs a person to confirm it, and can never be done by a schedule or an agent on its own. Face recognition is off unless you turn it on. Camera clips are encrypted, kept for days rather than months, and every viewing is logged.

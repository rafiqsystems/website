Rafiq Hikma

A study companion for any course, any school and any family. It turns lessons and documents into material to revise from, answers questions only from what was actually taught, and helps parents follow and plan their children's learning.

See how it works

Pick a step.

What it does

  • Transcripts, summaries, glossaries, flashcards and quizzes from lecture recordings and course documents.
  • A tutor that answers from your course material and cites the exact timestamp or page.
  • Scanned and handwritten pages read with OCR.
  • Pluggable engines for transcription, OCR, search and models, so a school can run everything on its own hardware or mix in cloud services it trusts.
  • Built for more than one language from the start.
  • For parents: each child's progress, what is due, and the topics that are not sticking yet, in one view.
  • Study plans made for the child from the syllabus, the school calendar and the family calendar, adjusted when a week goes badly.
  • Study guides and aids at the child's level: summaries, flashcards, practice quizzes, worked examples and audio recaps.

Your data

A school, a family or a student can host it themselves. Course material, notes and questions stay on that server unless a cloud provider is switched on, and each provider is a visible, revocable grant. A child sees exactly what a parent can see about them. There is no advertising, no profiling and nothing designed to hook a child.

Status

Phase 2. The ingestion pipeline and its provider interfaces are built and tested, with local and cloud engines behind the same interface. The features for parents are designed, not yet built.

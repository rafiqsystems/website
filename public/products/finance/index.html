Rafiq Finance

See every account, transaction and loan in one place, kept on your own device. Then ask it things: where the money goes, how to reach a goal sooner, and whether a purchase is worth it right now.

See how it works

Pick a step.

What it does

  • One ledger for bank accounts, cards, loans, recurring bills and net worth over time.
  • Transactions sorted for you by a model that runs on your device and learns from your corrections.
  • Budgets and goals with honest projections based on how you actually spend.
  • A forecast that warns you before a shortfall, not after.
  • Ask before you buy: a reasoned answer showing what a purchase does to this month and to your goals, the cost per use, and what waiting would change.
  • A household view for shared bills, with each person's private accounts kept private.

Your data

Your finances stay on your device, encrypted. Connections to banks are read-only by design: Rafiq can see your accounts and can never move your money. You can run it entirely from files you import and never connect a bank. It explains and reasons from your own numbers; it is not regulated financial advice and does not sell you products.

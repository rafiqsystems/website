Najid

One page that tells you what today looks like: your calendar, what needs attention in your inbox, open tasks from your notes, and a few headlines. Every line says where it came from.

See how it works

Pick a step.

What it does

  • A daily briefing, on request or on a schedule you set.
  • Calendar, mail headers, notes and news through connectors that each declare what they read and where they send it.
  • Scheduled runs only touch what you explicitly allowed to run unattended.
  • Suggestions are text. Acting on one, such as moving an event or replying, is a separate permission.
  • A missing permission produces a skipped section, never a nag.

Your data

Connectors contact only the hosts named on their consent card. Credentials live in the vault, never in the grant. Every call is written to an audit log you can read.

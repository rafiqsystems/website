Trademark policy

The code is free. The name "Rafiq", "Rafiq Systems" and the logo are not covered by the software licences.

You may use the name to refer to the project, to say that your software works with Rafiq, and in articles, talks and reviews. You may redistribute unmodified official builds under the name.

If you modify Rafiq and distribute it, or host a modified version, use a different name and logo so that users know what they are running. "Powered by Rafiq" style attribution is welcome.

This is a short-form policy for the foundation phase. A complete policy will be published here before the first release. Questions: hello@rafiqsystems.com.
